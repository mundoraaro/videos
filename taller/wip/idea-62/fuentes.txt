- IPS / Joseph Chamie, "Planet Earth's Increasing Population of 8 Billion" (mar 2026), cita a la ONU: población mundial 2026 = 8,300 millones.
- INEGI Censo 2020 (vía Wikipedia, "List of Mexican states by population density"): CDMX 9,209,944 hab. en 1,494.3 km² = 6,163 hab/km²; Chihuahua 247,412.6 km²; México 1,960,646.7 km². (Sonora ~179,355 km², Coahuila ~151,595 km², INEGI.)
- Cálculo: 8.3e9 / 6,163 = 1.35 millones de km² = 68.7 % de México (≈ dos terceras partes). Sobran ~614,000 km² > Chihuahua + Sonora + Coahuila (~578,000 km²).

- Cálculo: 8.3e9 / 43,064 = 192,700 km² < Chihuahua 247,413 km² (sobran ~55,000 km²).
